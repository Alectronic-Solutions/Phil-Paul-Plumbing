STOCKTON PLUMBING QUESTIONS

Water Heaters

We replace, move, install and repair all types of water heaters. Tankless water heaters are efficient, and take up less space. Traditional tank-type water heaters are much more efficient than they were 10 or more years ago and are still the number one preferred water heater in the industry. We have extensive knowledge of all types of water heaters and will be able to help you.

Drain Cleaning & Rooter Service

We will send out a skilled drain cleaning technician to take care of your drain cleaning needs and will put priority on an emergency drain problem. This includes sewer lines, kitchen shower, and tub drains, kitchen sink, toilet, floor drains, and landscape drains. We have the experience and commercial quality rooter machines that can handle the toughest of jobs.

Drain Pipe Repair & Replacement

We repair, replace and install all types of drain pipes. Corrosion is a common occurrence in older properties. Metal pipes age and rust forms slowing the water flow, eroding the metal, and increasing the chance of corrosion and leaking pipes. We will handle the problem by installing new ABS piping that will not rust or corrode, backed by our guarantee.
